1) Cardi B made her first NPR Tiny Desk appearance on October 19, moving through songs including “Bodak Yellow,” “Be Careful,” “Up” and “I Like It.” The performance puts her catalog back in focus as the Bronx rapper’s next album cycle starts to take shape. @iamcardib

2) Lil Jon’s memoir, I Only Shout So You Can Hear Me, arrives October 20 through Atria/Black Privilege Publishing. The book traces his Atlanta rise, crunk persona and the childhood eye injury that inspired his signature shades, adding context to the image behind the hype. @liljon

3) D’Angelo’s brother Luther Archer says the family is preparing one final posthumous album from the artist’s vault. Unreleased material is being considered, including a potential Michael Jackson collaboration; no release date has been announced, so this remains an estate plan rather than a finished album. @Rapwire247

4) 50 Cent’s Street Fighter opened No. 1 in North America with $31 million from 3,575 theaters and $51.1 million worldwide. He plays Balrog in the film, giving the rap veteran another major screen role as the video-game adaptation begins its theatrical run. @50cent

5) The Supreme Court declined to hear a BMG-led challenge involving songwriters reclaiming worldwide copyrights, leaving the Fifth Circuit’s decision in place within its circuit. Because the justices did not decide the merits, the ruling is not a nationwide precedent, but it keeps the creator-rights issue alive for music publishers and writers. @Rapwire247
