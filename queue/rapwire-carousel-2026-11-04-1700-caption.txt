1) On Nov. 3, the Bucharest Court of Appeal granted Wiz Khalifa’s annulment challenge, voided the nine-month prison sentence tied to his 2024 Beach, Please! cannabis case, and canceled the execution warrant. That does not equal an acquittal: DIICOT prosecutors’ appeal is scheduled for a new hearing on Nov. 12, with video participation being arranged. @wizkhalifa’s legal status is reopened, not resolved. @Rapwire247

2) Abdul El-Sayed’s final Michigan Senate ad, released on Election Day, is soundtracked by Eminem’s 2010 hit “Not Afraid.” The track appears alongside the Democrat’s get-out-the-vote message, but a representative for Eminem said the campaign did not contact him about using it, so the clip should not be read as a confirmed endorsement. It lands weeks after Eminem appeared on Kid Rock’s “Heal My Faith.” @eminem @Rapwire247

3) Belly Gang Kushington revealed GOOD DOPE SELL ITSELF, his sophomore album, for Nov. 13 and paired it with the first WTF GOIN Tour, with Atlanta, Detroit and Houston dates from Nov. 25 through Dec. 8. The rollout follows “WTF GOIN” with 21 Savage, his first Billboard Hot 100 breakthrough and a 2026 run that also brought XXL Freshman recognition. The album is the next test of whether that breakout can travel beyond one hit. @bellygangkushington @Rapwire247

4) Wu-Tang Clan will headline the Detroit Lions’ Thanksgiving Day halftime show on Nov. 26 when Detroit hosts the Chicago Bears at Ford Field. The game is set for national CBS coverage, and Eminem plus Paul Rosenberg are returning as executive producers who select the talent. The booking comes as Wu-Tang closes its farewell tour and heads into its Rock & Roll Hall of Fame induction. @wutangclan @Rapwire247

5) Rockstar says digital pre-load for GTA VI begins Nov. 10, while the console install needs about 212 GB ahead of the Nov. 19 launch. That gives players a concrete final countdown and a serious storage check before Jason and Lucia’s Vice City-era world opens on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. @rockstargames @Rapwire247
