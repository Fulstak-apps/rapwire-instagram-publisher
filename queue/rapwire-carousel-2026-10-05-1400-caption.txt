1) The Game (@losangelesconfidential) and his company 100 Entertainment sued Universal Music Group and Virgin Music Group over the rollout of his 2022 album Drillmatic — Heart vs. Mind. The complaint says a promised $500,000 marketing fund was largely left unspent and seeks at least $500,000 in damages; those are allegations, not a court finding. The case matters because it puts label-distribution promises and album marketing accountability back in the spotlight.

2) GloRilla (@glorillapimp) launched GloVerse, a beta platform that connects clients with beauty professionals for on-demand hair, makeup, braiding, nails, lashes and more. The web service is live now, while a fuller iOS and Android app is planned for later in 2026. It marks a concrete expansion of the Memphis rapper’s business identity beyond music.

3) Ye joined Playboi Carti (@playboicarti) onstage at ComplexCon’s Los Angeles finale for “Can’t Tell Me Nothing,” “Carnival” and “Off the Grid.” Destroy Lonely and Ken Carson also appeared during Carti’s closing set on Oct. 4, turning the 10th-anniversary festival’s final night into a surprise Opium-era link-up. The appearance is confirmed; any new project talk remains unannounced.

4) André 3000 made a rare ComplexCon appearance on the festival’s closing day in Los Angeles, nearly a decade after his last visit. The newly inducted Rock & Roll Hall of Famer was spotted linking with Jeff Staple during the event’s 10th-anniversary weekend. It is a culture-and-fashion sighting, not an announcement of new music.

5) A now-removed PEGI listing gave Grand Theft Auto VI an 18 rating and described graphic violence including decapitation and dismemberment, frequent drug use and strong sexual themes. GamesRadar and PC Gamer both reported the unusually detailed wording, while Rockstar’s official release date remains Nov. 19, 2026 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The listing signals how firmly the next GTA is being positioned for adults.
