1) @nickcannon is taking Nick Cannon @ Night from its Wondery-born advice format to BET. The network says the series premieres October 20 at 10 p.m. ET and will run weekly for 10 episodes, with viewer questions and guests guiding conversations about love, dating and health. The move gives Cannon another broadcast late-night lane after the podcast built its audience online.

2) New testimony in the Durban High Court trial over the 2023 murders of @akaworldwide and Tebello “Tibz” Motsoane focused on the rapper’s security detail. Witnesses said the bodyguard ran inside when gunshots were heard, while the manager said AKA’s regular guard was unavailable for the trip. Seven men remain on trial and all have pleaded not guilty.

3) Deezer is adding a user-controlled toggle that removes fully synthetic tracks, albums and their associated artists from search results. The feature is planned for worldwide rollout before the end of 2026, building on the platform’s detection and labeling system. Deezer says it has already identified more than 42 million such tracks and believes the option gives listeners more control over what reaches them.

4) @iamluckydaye says a management change pushed him to take more control ahead of ALOUD!, his Warner Records album. The 15-track project arrives October 16 with Pusha T, Leon Thomas, Muni Long and others, following his label shift from RCA. His own framing of the record is about rejecting permission and making room for a freer next chapter.

5) A new deep-dive into GTA VI traces more than eight years of work through the Red Dead Redemption 2 detour, the 2022 leak and major changes inside Rockstar. The project began in 2015 as a broader Project Americas idea before narrowing into Vice City and Leonida, while Rockstar grew to more than 4,700 people across 12 offices. The announced November 19, 2026 console date remains unchanged; the new reporting is about how the game was made, not a new release-date announcement.
