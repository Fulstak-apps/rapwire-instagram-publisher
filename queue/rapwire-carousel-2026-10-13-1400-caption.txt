1) @dontoliver is on the 2027 Grammy Best New Artist ballot after five straight top-10 albums dating to 2020. The entry list has 331 acts, and voters will choose eight nominees by Nov. 16; Octane gave Toliver his first No. 1 on the Billboard 200. The category’s rules allow a long catalog when a screening panel sees the breakthrough as happening in the eligibility year.

2) @jayz’s JAY-Z30 pop-up is open at 7007 Romaine Street in Los Angeles through Oct. 22, selling anniversary jackets, tees and related pieces. The space leads into his Oct. 23 and 24 SoFi Stadium shows, which celebrate 30 years of Reasonable Doubt. The L.A. stop turns the anniversary from a concert run into a citywide retail-and-archive moment.

3) Mackayla “Kash” Paige, 29, was fatally shot in downtown Oakland early Oct. 11, and police arrested a Richmond woman in connection with the case. Family and local outlets identified Paige as a reality-TV performer known from Real West Coast Baddies and Young & Reckless; police have not released a motive or the suspect’s name. A growing memorial is now at the scene while homicide investigators ask for information.

4) An industry report says Rockstar may bring GTA VI to PC in Q1 2027, only months after the Nov. 19 console launch. Rockstar has not confirmed that window, so it remains a report rather than an announced date. If it holds, PC players could see a much shorter wait than GTA V’s roughly 18-month console-to-PC gap.

5) Cyberleek is claiming a purported full GTA VI build will be released if its memecoin reaches a $30 million market cap, while demanding physical discs and a public apology from Take-Two. Reporting on the threat has not established that the leaker controls a complete build, and Rockstar and Take-Two have not validated the claim. The verified part is the continuing leak campaign and legal/takedown context, so fans should avoid spoilers, downloads and linked token schemes.
