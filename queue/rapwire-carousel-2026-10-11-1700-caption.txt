1) Nivea said on The Tamron Hall Show that her chronic myeloid leukemia is in remission after treatment. She also said she is still dealing with side effects and adjusting to the diagnosis, while holding onto music, motherhood and gratitude.

2) The Prince Estate released the four-track Calabama EP with the original “Vault Raw” version, the Timeless album mix and two previously unreleased Prince And The Band recordings. The release follows criticism over horns added after Prince’s death and gives listeners a direct comparison of the archival choices. @prince

3) K. Michelle’s first country album, Jesus & Whiskey, is due Oct. 16 through BBR Music Group and Real Outlawz Records. She is marking the release with three nights in New Orleans, Dallas and Houston, mixing the new songs with the R&B catalog that made her a star. @kmichellemusic

4) Offset and Don Toliver’s new single Every Night flips Jodeci’s 1995 classic Freek’n You and arrives ahead of Offset’s GMFU album, due Oct. 23 on Motown. The collaboration follows their earlier links on Worth It and Had Enough while giving the rollout a glossy casino-video edge. @offsetyrn @dontoliver

5) Rockstar’s Rob Nelson says GTA VI treats Jason and Lucia’s relationship as an optional system that grows through shared activities, texts and time in the open world. Players can ignore the romance layer without breaking the main story, and the game remains scheduled for Nov. 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
