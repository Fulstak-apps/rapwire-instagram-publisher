1) Skepta’s The Fork and Knife arrived Nov. 6 through Big Smoke Records and Epic, marking his first solo album since 2019’s Ignorance Is Bliss. The 15-song project closes a seven-year gap and gives the grime veteran a full-length return with a fresh guest-heavy frame. @skepta

2) Ms. Lauryn Hill and Wyclef Jean launched Fugees Hopeline with CIRCA, an interactive phone experience that gives callers a first listen to new Fugees music. The project turns the group’s legacy into a living art piece, with Hill’s daughter Selah Marley narrating the experience and the music delivered through a hotline. @laurynhill @wyclef

3) Snoop Dogg has a new animated visual for “Lodi Dodi,” the Doggystyle classic, directed by Toronto-born animator Tristan Zammit, known as Tristious. The clip puts a cartoon Snoop, studio performance and hand-drawn storytelling in conversation with one of the defining songs from his catalog. @SnoopDogg

4) Tay-K announced #MonstersUnderMyBed for Nov. 13, his first full-length project since 2017’s #SantanaWorld. The album was assembled over three years through prison phone sessions, and the rollout includes the SoundCloud-only single “F**K U”; he remains incarcerated while serving concurrent sentences tied to two fatal cases. @tayk47shawty

5) Rockstar says Grand Theft Auto VI digital pre-loading begins Nov. 10 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, nine days before the Nov. 19 launch. Current product reporting puts the download at up to 212 GB, making storage a real part of launch prep for Jason and Lucia’s return to Vice City. @RockstarGames @Rapwire247
