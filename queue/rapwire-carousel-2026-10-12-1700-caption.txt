1) @Diddy was taken from FCI Fort Dix for treatment after a reported head injury and returned to the facility before sunrise. His representative said the circumstances were still being confirmed, while the cause and exact hospital remained unclear. The incident is the latest health update around his federal sentence, but no life-threatening condition was reported.

2) @Gunna launched SB THE LABEL with Atlantic Music Group and named Casper Bluff, Nechie and Dracc as its first signings. He is taking the CEO role while rolling out “pop it,” whose video features Coco Gauff, and preparing the next chapter around Splurge. The move puts artist development and a new label roster at the center of Gunna’s new chapter.

3) @paulwallbaby learned his mother had died while he was on the way to his Texas State Fair show, but he still took the stage because she was proud of the booking. He performed at the Paul Wall Fall Ball in Dallas and honored her before leaving the stage. His post turned a scheduled appearance into a public tribute.

4) @realshyne said on Drink Champs that Jay-Z planned to sign him after prison, until a leaked DJ Khaled recording exposed how much his voice had changed. Shyne said he was not ready to deliver at the time; he is now preparing a 2622 LGND Tour return with Barrington Levy and Lil’ Cease. The story is a look at a major rap “what if” that never became a deal.

5) @rockstargames says GTA VI has a dedicated Los Angeles team writing pedestrian dialogue, with advertising and comedy writers contributing from New York, Los Angeles and London. The point is to make overheard street life feel authored rather than disposable background noise. That detail helps explain why Leonida is being built as a world that keeps performing between missions.
