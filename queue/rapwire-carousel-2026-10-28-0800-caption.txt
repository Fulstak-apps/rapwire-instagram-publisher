1) Jay-Z (@jayz) will headline the Super Bowl LXI halftime show on Feb. 14, 2027, at SoFi Stadium in Inglewood. The NFL, Apple Music and Roc Nation made the announcement after years of Jay-Z helping shape the show through Roc Nation. It is a major shift from executive producer to featured performer.

2) Songwriter Aviel Hirschfield voluntarily dismissed his copyright case over Drake’s “Janice STFU” without prejudice. That ends this lawsuit without a ruling on whether the song’s authorship claims were valid, although the complaint could technically be filed again. Drake’s team had denied the allegations.

3) Young Nudy (@youngnudy) released Just Doin Me on Oct. 28, with Apple Music listing 17 songs on the current release page. The Atlanta rapper built the rollout around the lead single “Who Dat Is,” giving fans a new full-length chapter in his 2026 run.

4) Macklemore (@mrmacklemore) opened his three-date Free Palestine benefit run in Dublin with Hozier, Kneecap and Beoga joining the bill. Proceeds are slated for Palestinian aid groups after Macklemore was dropped from Ed Sheeran’s tour following onstage political comments. Paris and London are next.

5) Rockstar says physical copies of Grand Theft Auto VI arrive Nov. 12 with a download code inside for pre-loading, ahead of the Nov. 19 launch on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The date and format are confirmed, while rumor-only PC claims remain unconfirmed.
