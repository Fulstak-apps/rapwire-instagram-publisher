1) Metro Boomin’s new ONLYHANDS game made its first public unveiling at New York Comic Con. The original 1v1 fighting game turns real recording artists into playable characters, with Metro serving as Season 1 cover artist and executive producer as the project expands his world beyond records. @MetroBoomin

2) Nas and Slick Rick brought hip-hop history into Marvel territory at New York Comic Con. The pair joined artist Sanford Greene around the forthcoming Legend Has It Vol. 2 crossover, a Mass Appeal project that reimagines foundational New York artists through superhero storytelling. @Nas

3) A Manhattan judge is testing how far 50 Cent’s G-Unit Books life-rights deal can reach. The court questioned whether the roughly $1 million agreement with Shaniqua Tompkins could silence her own life story forever; the dispute remains active, with the sides scheduled to return Dec. 15. @50cent

4) Jay-Z’s long-running fight with Tony Buzbee took another procedural turn. A California appeals court denied rehearing, while a modified opinion left a path for a lower court to revisit the dispute after an accuser’s recantation; the ruling does not decide the underlying allegations. @jayz

5) Lauryn Hill and Wyclef Jean are taking The Score’s 30th anniversary to New York Comic Con. The Fugees collaborators are part of a limited superhero-inspired art print and anniversary merchandise rollout tied to Mass Appeal and Whatnot’s hip-hop showcase, connecting a landmark album to a new collectible culture. @MsLaurynHill @WyclefJean
