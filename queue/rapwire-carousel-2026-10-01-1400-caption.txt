1) Rick Ross surrendered to Miami Beach police Thursday and faces one felony battery-by-strangulation charge and one misdemeanor battery charge tied to an ex-girlfriend’s accusation. He was released on bond, has pleaded not guilty, and is due in court October 22; his attorney says he is innocent. @richforever

2) Drake said a selection of songs from his hour-long FOMO short film would reach streaming services October 1, including unreleased collaborations and a new Don Toliver remix. The complete tracklist is still unconfirmed, making the drop another surprise chapter in his run of 2026 releases. @champagnepapi

3) Quavo’s 14-track QRÖMELIFE arrives October 2 with Pharrell producing every song and guest spots from Offset, Future, Tyler, the Creator and A$AP Rocky. The release also points toward a final Migos album, giving the solo rollout a larger family-and-legacy frame. @quavohuncho

4) Grand Theft Auto VI storefronts are showing Rating Pending and provisional Mature 17+ markers, while new listings surface adult-content warnings and in-game purchases. The final ESRB certificate is not public yet, but the labels confirm that Rockstar is marketing the game for an adult audience ahead of its November 19 launch. @RockstarGames

5) Rockstar told Game Informer that GTA VI will not recreate Orlando’s famous theme-park corridor, including direct versions of Disney World and Universal. The studio says the locations it kept are still designed to deliver a full Florida experience across Leonida’s fictional map. @RockstarGames
