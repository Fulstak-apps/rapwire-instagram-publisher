1) Cardi B will receive Billboard Canada’s first-ever Global Woman of the Year award at the November 24 Women in Music celebration in Toronto. The honor follows her sold-out Little Miss Drama arena run and 46 Canadian Hot 100 chart entries, including eight top-10 hits, adding another global milestone to @iamcardib’s record book.

2) Gunna’s Wunna Run Club and Walmart will award $10,000 in scholarships to five Florida A&M University students pursuing majors with a health-related component. Each recipient is set to receive $2,000 through the October 22 Homecoming 5K at Bragg Memorial Stadium, extending @gunna’s wellness campaign into direct HBCU support.

3) A$AP Rocky is taking the Don’t Be Dumb World Tour to Australia and New Zealand in December, with arena dates in Melbourne, Sydney, Auckland, Brisbane and Perth. The run marks his first trip Down Under in eight years and follows a sold-out 25-date Europe and U.K. leg for @asvpxrocky.

4) Doechii has returned to her Swamp Sessions series with “BOMBS,” a short, high-energy track and self-directed video that marks her first solo release of 2026. In a handwritten letter to fans, she framed this chapter around experimentation and putting Black womanhood at the center of hip-hop and pop. @doechii

5) Grand Theft Auto VI is still being adjusted as Rockstar squashes bugs and tunes the build ahead of November 19. Technology director Phil Hooker said the team never has an exact blueprint and has changed parts of the game even in recent months, a glimpse at the scale of the studio’s final push. @Rapwire247
