1) Chance the Rapper (@chancetherapper) said on Tea Time that he is considering dropping “Chance” and going simply by “The Rapper.” He tied the idea to wanting his public identity to center on craft, language and the genre rather than personal vulnerability while his Coloring Book anniversary tour continues. The change is not official yet, but the interview puts a possible rebrand on the record.

2) Yung Miami (@yungmiami) announced Watch Dis, her debut solo album, for Oct. 23 and launched it with “F You” and “2008,” plus a video for the latter. She framed the 12-track project as a personal bet after being counted out, following the solo momentum of “Spend Dat.” This is the former City Girls rapper’s first full-length solo statement.

3) Eminem (@eminem) will appear on Kid Rock’s “7 Sins,” a song from Halfway to Jesus, Kid Rock’s 17-track Christian album due Oct. 17. Kid Rock says they want friendship across political differences to speak louder than the split, making the Detroit-rooted reunion a bigger conversation than a guest verse. The collaboration has been announced, but the track itself is still upcoming.

4) Rockstar’s LOVE feature says GTA VI is leaning into satire that is bigger and more grotesque than a simple mirror of reality, with radio, ads, pedestrian dialogue and even robo-voiced political calls built into Vice City. Rockstar writers say the world is designed to surround the player with overlapping conversations while grounding Jason and Lucia in research-heavy Florida-inspired worldbuilding. The game remains scheduled for Nov. 19, 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

5) Producer Digital Nas says he received no royalties or publishing from work across Ye’s Donda and Vultures projects, announced that he was stepping away from music, then said the dispute was “getting handled.” His claim is not an adjudicated finding, and Ye/YZY has not publicly confirmed the account. The development revives a larger contract problem previously reported around Vultures, where more than 10 producers lacked signed agreements.
