1) Conway the Machine released OTIS on Nov. 20 through Drumwork and Roc Nation Distribution. The 11-song album brings Ghostface Killah, Raekwon and Method Man onto “Lupita!” and extends Conway’s independent run with veteran chemistry at the center. @whoisconway

2) The 2027 Grammys give Best Rap Album nominations to Busta Rhymes & J Dilla, Cardi B, Rapsody, Don Toliver, Wale and Ye, while rap is absent from Record, Album and Song of the Year. The ceremony is scheduled for Feb. 7 in Los Angeles, putting the genre’s category strength beside a conspicuously thin general-field showing. @iamcardib @rapsody @dontoliver

3) Slim Thug brought King of Cars back to Houston on Nov. 22 at Reliant Center after nearly a decade away. The one-day festival combines custom slabs, vendors and a hometown rap lineup featuring Lil’ Keke, Paul Wall, Z-Ro and more, making the event a local culture statement as much as a car show. @slimthug

4) Master P surprised Usher during the Nov. 21 New Orleans stop of the R&B Tour at Caesars Superdome and presented him with a No Limit Soldier chain. The moment linked Usher and Chris Brown’s arena run to the city’s rap history and No Limit’s hometown legacy. @masterp @usher

5) More than 1,600 UK Rockstar employees are voting on union recognition through Nov. 26. A win could create the largest games union in Britain, while the ballot unfolds during GTA VI’s launch window and amid a tribunal fight over alleged union-busting. @RockstarGames
