1) Mike Jones is seeing a reported 700% jump in streams for “Still Tippin’” and “Back Then” after both Houston classics landed on Grand Theft Auto VI’s radio soundtrack. The spike is pulling chopped-and-screwed Houston rap back into the conversation and introducing the 2000s sound to players who were too young to catch it the first time. @where_mikejones

2) Skepta is credited on all four songs at the top of the Official U.K. Rap Chart for Nov. 20–26. “Broken Hearts” with Central Cee and Jorja Smith leads, followed by “Currency,” “Chiltern Firehouse” with Fakemink and “Revenge,” making the sweep a major chart statement for the grime veteran. @skepta

3) GloRilla’s Big Glo’s 901 Turkey Drive is scheduled for Nov. 24 at Smackers Restaurant in Memphis, with doors opening at 3 p.m. and turkeys available while supplies last. The hometown rapper’s latest giveaway follows her $25,000 donation to Cordova High School and extends a visible run of community support. @glorillapimp

4) The Second Circuit upheld the dismissal of Tuff City Records’ copyright appeal over Mary J. Blige’s “Real Love.” The court said shared percussion with “Impeach the President” did not make the songs substantially similar, so Universal’s win stays in place and Blige was not a party to the case. @therealmaryjblige

5) Rockstar’s first post-launch GTA VI update for PS5 and Xbox fixes crashes, weather that stayed unchanged too long, broken autosaves and phone calls that failed to trigger story content. It also addresses a stuck Wanted Level, a gym deadlift bug and missing handoffs at fences, making the patch relevant to both polish and progression. @rockstargames
