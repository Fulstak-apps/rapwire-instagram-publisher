1) Rick Ross (@richforever) is no longer facing the felony battery-by-strangulation count in his Miami Beach case after Miami-Dade prosecutors cited insufficient evidence and dropped it. He still faces a misdemeanor domestic-violence battery charge and has pleaded not guilty, so the case is not over. The change lowers the most serious count while the remaining allegation proceeds.
2) Megan Thee Stallion (@theestallion) released Tina Snow Freestyles on Oct. 30, a three-track EP collecting “Tina Tuesday,” “Is You Cool” and “Thin Ice.” The project packages the Houston rapper’s recent freestyle run under her Tina Snow persona as she continues work toward another album expected later this year.
3) 2 Chainz (@2chainz) confirmed that P.O.L.O., produced entirely by Statik Selektah, arrives Dec. 4 after the pair dropped “A Week in Paris” on Oct. 30. The track formalizes a project fans had been anticipating since last year and positions the veteran rapper in a focused producer-led era.
4) LL COOL J (@llcoolj) announced Rock the Bells: The Residency, an 11-show Las Vegas run at Dolby Live at Park MGM from May 15 through July 17, 2027. The shows will pair his catalog with DJs, immersive storytelling and rotating guests, with presales beginning Oct. 30 and general tickets Nov. 2.
5) Rockstar’s latest Grand Theft Auto VI preview says the studio art-directed about 600,000 NPC animations and gave pedestrians personality traits that influence how they react to the player and the world around them. The report describes a Los Angeles-based team of writers, directors and producers building more individualized Leonida crowds ahead of the Nov. 19 launch. This matters because the NPC system is one of the clearest new details about how GTA VI aims to make its open world feel reactive rather than decorative.
