1) A$AP Rocky (@asaprocky) announced a four-year full-tuition scholarship for one first-time freshman at the Roc Nation School of Music, Sports & Entertainment at Long Island University. Applications are open through Dec. 15, 2026 for Fall 2027, with mentorship and hands-on industry access built into the award.

2) BET announced the 2026 Soul Train Awards nominees and opened fan voting in two new categories, including R&B/Soul Song of the Year and Spotify’s R&B X International Artist. Voting runs through Oct. 16, with the ceremony set for Nov. 27 across BET and CBS-led simulcasts.

3) BigXthaPlug (@bigxthaplug) revealed his fourth album, BETTER OVER TIME, for Oct. 30, following a nationwide billboard rollout across artists’ hometowns. The project is led by “LITE” with Belly Gang Kushington and includes Lil Yachty, GloRilla, Ava Max, Leon Thomas, Loe Shimmy, Ro$ama, Fridayy and Babyface Ray.

4) Doja Cat (@dojacat) has started the North American leg of her Tour Ma Vie World Tour, with Latto (@latto777) booked as special guest on every date. The 31-city run opened Oct. 1 in Detroit, reaches Kansas City on Oct. 6, and closes Dec. 1 at Madison Square Garden.

5) Former Rockstar co-founder Dan Houser says he is deliberately avoiding GTA VI and other open-world games so they do not distract from his work at Absurd Ventures. His studio is developing an open-world comedy and a dystopian sci-fi game, making the comment a revealing look at his post-Rockstar creative direction.
