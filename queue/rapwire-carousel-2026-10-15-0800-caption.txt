1) Drakeo the Ruler’s estate has reached a conditional settlement with Live Nation, Hollywood Elite Security and Black Tie Events over the rapper’s fatal backstage stabbing at the 2021 Once Upon a Time in L.A. festival. The terms are undisclosed, and claims brought by Drakeo’s son and brother are still set for trial next month, so the case is not fully over.

2) Megan Thee Stallion is Gymshark’s first celebrity ambassador in a multiyear partnership launched through the “Megan Does Gym” campaign. Gymshark’s official page says the red SoftSense “Thee Red Shorts” are scheduled to drop October 22, turning her training routine into a new retail lane alongside her music career. @theestallion

3) Universal Music Group is in a restructuring wave across its U.S. and U.K. operations, with senior exits tied to Def Jam, Interscope, Republic and UMG’s U.K. and Irish businesses. Variety reported Tunji Balogun will stay through year-end at Def Jam, while Warner confirmed former Republic chief Jim Roppo as its incoming vice chairman; the shift matters because it touches the label teams steering major rap rosters.

4) Rockstar built a dedicated Los Angeles operation of writers, producers and directors to handle GTA VI’s enormous NPC and pedestrian-dialogue workload, according to new reporting from Bloomberg and PC Gamer. The move helps explain why the game’s scope pushed its schedule and why Leonida’s background characters are being treated as authored worldbuilding rather than filler; Rockstar’s announced console date remains November 19, 2026.

5) GamesBeat reports that Rockstar is targeting a Q1 2027 PC release for GTA VI, citing four industry sources familiar with the studio’s plans. Rockstar has officially announced the November 19, 2026 launch for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, but it has not confirmed a PC date, so the report is a window to watch—not a release announcement.
