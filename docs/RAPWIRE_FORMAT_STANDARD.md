# RapWire 24/7 format standard

Every post begins by choosing one format. A source screenshot is never a finished format.

## 1. Video repost

Use when the approved-source item is playable: trailer, music video, interview, clip, or Reel.

- Download the actual video and its audio; never publish a screenshot or thumbnail in its place.
- Remove only outer platform chrome or a source-logo strip that is entirely separate from the footage. Never crop faces, subtitles, a trailer title, a tweet, or material inside the video.
- Put the source video in one centered, complete frame. Use blurred background only to fill unused space.
- Keep the video within Instagram's center-square grid safe area (y=135–1215 on a 1080x1350 post).
- Use a compact RapWire 24/7 bug once, below the picture or in a clean unused corner. Never place it over a face, subtitle, headline, or tweet.
- Put the RapWire-written factual caption in the dedicated bottom panel, never over the source video. The Instagram/Threads post caption carries the longer context.
- Keep third-party attribution in the written post caption; omit credit only for user-owned accounts @trapmatictv and @raplisted_.

## 2. Tweet / statement

Use when the news is the exact words of a tweet, X post, statement, court exhibit, or social-post text.

- Show the original statement at readable scale, essentially full-width, with no decorative boxes, frames, headline panels, artist tags, or border treatments over it.
- Crop only platform UI outside the statement. Do not crop the author, post text, timestamp, quoted post, or engagement context when it affects the meaning.
- A small RapWire bug is allowed below the statement, not on it.
- Add a separate clean detail slide only when verified context is needed. Do not cover the statement with a rewritten headline.
- Do not add a blog's @handle to the asset. Attribute the original author in the caption if needed.

## 3. Photo / breaking-news carousel

Use only when a photo is genuinely the story and no playable source video or primary statement is the stronger format.

- Slide 1: one clean source image or approved original editorial artwork plus a short factual headline. No visible blog header, handle, repost watermark, or screenshot chrome.
- Slide 2+: concise verified context. Split copy into more slides rather than shrinking it or cutting sentences.
- Show handles only for a verified artist/public figure central to the news. Never display a blog/source account handle in the graphic.
- Keep branding compact and consistent; no oversized badge padding and no border for border's sake.

## Non-negotiable QA

- Preview the 1080x1350 post and Instagram's center 1080x1080 grid crop before publishing.
- Text, faces, tweets, subtitles, and trailer titles must remain fully visible in both previews.
- Never use a screenshot where a real video is available.
- Never mix formats: a tweet should look like a tweet; a trailer should play as a trailer; a news photo should look like a clean news image.
- Do not post general-pop or unrelated content. Rap and hip-hop are the default; gaming is occasional.
