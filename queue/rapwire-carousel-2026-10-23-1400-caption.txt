1) Shabazz Palaces’ It’s a Style Sport is now available through Sub Pop. Ishmael Butler’s 10-track return was shaped in an eight-day Seattle session with longtime collaborators, making the album a fresh statement from one of rap’s most exploratory voices.

2) Che’ Noir’s It Takes A Village is out now as a 12-track statement through Poetic Records and HiPNOTT Records. Black Thought, Ransom and Bruiser Wolf join a production roster that includes Havoc, Nottz and Conductor Williams, giving the Buffalo MC a deep cross-generational circle around her writing.

3) Hit-Boy has released The Chauncey Hollis Project II, a nine-song sequel named for the producer-rapper’s birth name and issued through Surf Club. The Alchemist, 03 Greedo, Dom Kennedy and Stacy Barthe appear across a set that leans into introspection instead of chasing a radio-sized hook.

4) Warner Music Group has unified its global sync operations across recorded music and publishing, with Ron Broitman leading the new structure. The company says the goal is a faster path for its catalog into film, television, advertising and games, a lane that can expand how rap records reach visual audiences.

5) New reporting on Rockstar’s GTA VI development says map work began around 2015, with artists building a denser world around roads narrower than those in GTA V. The game remains scheduled for Nov. 19 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, so every new design detail sharpens the picture of what the long build was buying.
