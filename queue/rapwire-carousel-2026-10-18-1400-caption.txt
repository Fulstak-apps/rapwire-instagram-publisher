1. Drake @champagnepapi is facing a copyright lawsuit from songwriter and producer Aviel Calev Hirschfield, who says the chorus of “Janice STFU” matches a demo he sent through Drake’s collaborators. Drake’s team has categorically denied the claim, while the song remains one of his defining 2026 hits and a major chart leader.

2. Eminem @eminem is defending his “Heal My Faith” collaboration with longtime friend Kid Rock after fans questioned the political tension around the pairing. The verse appears on Kid Rock’s new album Halfway 2 Jesus, and Eminem says the decision was about making music with an old friend—not choosing sides.

3. Vybz Kartel @vybzkartel has put a December 18 preorder date behind his next project, The Sun. Apple Music lists it as a pre-release album from Vybz Kartel Muzik and Silver Birds Records, but no full tracklist or feature rollout has been announced yet.

4. Yung Miami @YungMiami305 said she has not spoken with former City Girls partner JT in about two years, but she did not close the door on recording together again. While promoting her solo debut Watch Dis, Miami answered “Inshallah” when asked about a reunion, keeping the possibility alive without announcing a plan.

5. Rockstar Games @rockstargames has released the Grand Theft Auto VI launch trailer, bringing Jason and Lucia’s relationship and the game’s crime-fueled Leonida into sharper focus. The trailer was captured in-game on PlayStation 5 and arrives ahead of the scheduled November 19 release, with a new Justice-led song adding to the rollout.
