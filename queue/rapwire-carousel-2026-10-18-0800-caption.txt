1) MMMG filed a federal lawsuit against Cordae, EMPIRE, Apeshit and Mass Appeal, alleging a 2018 recording deal still covers his music. The complaint seeks at least $25 million across contract and copyright claims and asks the court to stop the October 23 release of Heavy Is the Crown with Anderson .Paak. No judge has ruled on the claims or granted an injunction. @cordae

2) Lil Baby has set his next album, Fresh, for November 6 and shared a 17-song tracklist tease. Future and Young Thug are listed on “All My Patnas,” Rylo Rodriguez appears on “In Your Head,” and Pharrell-produced “Dead Fresh” is also on the board. The date turns a long-teased project into a defined rollout for @lilbaby_1.

3) Gucci Mane released Against All Odds on October 16 through 1017 and Atlantic Music Group. The 17-track album is his 18th studio LP and features Summer Walker, OJ da Juiceman and Ice Davis, extending a catalog that has shaped Atlanta trap for more than two decades. @gucci1017

4) ScHoolboy Q told fans that his 12-song album THank Que is coming in 2027 while celebrating the tenth anniversary of Blank Face LP on tour. He also promised he will still be around for the record's twentieth anniversary, making the announcement both a release update and a clear retirement rebuttal. @groovyq

5) Rockstar says it changed how GTA VI is made after the long-hours culture surrounding Red Dead Redemption 2. Head of publishing Jennifer Kolbe described paid time off or added pay for extra hours, while the studio continues tuning the build for its November 19 launch. The shift matters because GTA VI is Rockstar's biggest public test of that workplace reset. @Rapwire247
