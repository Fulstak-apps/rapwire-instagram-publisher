1) Lil Nas X (@lilnasx) is back with “World’s Greatest,” his first release since March 2025. The Atlanta-shot video connects his present-day comeback to childhood memories and dedicates the song to his late mother, Tamikia Hill. It is a personal reset after a long public pause.

2) Mac Miller’s estate released The Divine Feminine (10th Anniversary) with three previously unreleased session tracks: “Grand,” “Channel 13” and “Butterflies.” The rollout also includes The Divine Feminine Live, a special built from the 2016 performance series, giving fans new archival material while keeping the original album at the center.

3) The Koreatown Oddity’s new Stones Throw album Purdy turns family life, work, groceries and Los Angeles memory into 15 self-produced tracks. Dominique Purdy’s fourth album is a present-tense autobiography that makes everyday life the subject instead of chasing a trend.

4) Rob49 (@rob49up) released “Girls Girl” on Oct. 2 with a music video, continuing the run-up to his fifth studio album, Life of the Party. The New Orleans rapper follows “In the Club” with Sexyy Red and “Yes Ma’am,” keeping the rollout focused on momentum and his connection with fans.

5) Playboi Carti (@playboicarti) serves as artistic director and closes ComplexCon’s 10th-anniversary Los Angeles weekend on Oct. 4 with Destroy Lonely and F1lthy. The 00pium takeover makes the convention’s fashion, product and live-music sides part of one Carti-led statement.
