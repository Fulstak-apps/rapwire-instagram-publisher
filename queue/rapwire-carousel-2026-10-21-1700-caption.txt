1) Lil Baby announced FRESH for Nov. 6 through Quality Control and Motown, with a 17-track set that puts Future, Young Thug and Rylo Rodriguez together on one track. The album follows WHAM and The Leaks, and its tracklist and preorders are now public. @lilbaby_1 @future @thuggerthugger1 @rylorodriguez

2) Spotify and Believe announced a licensing deal for an upcoming paid fan-remix and cover tool covering participating Believe and TuneCore artists. Artists must opt in twice, and streams from licensed reworks are supposed to pay the original artists and rights holders; Spotify has not announced a launch date or price. @spotify @believemusic

3) Sean “Diddy” Combs could be left to handle his $100 million defamation suit himself after Sher Tremonte sought permission to withdraw over disputed bills and months without contact. No replacement lawyer has been named, the withdrawal is still awaiting a ruling, and the judge paused evidence-sharing while the representation issue is sorted out. @diddy

4) Bruno Mars’ “I Just Might” returned to No. 1 on Billboard’s Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart dated Oct. 24 and reached a record 32 weeks at the top. The track passed Kendrick Lamar and SZA’s “Luther,” after logging 5.03 million official streams, 51.9 million radio impressions and 1,000 U.S. sales in the tracking week. @brunomars

5) New reporting from Bloomberg’s Jason Schreier says some Rockstar artists have worked on GTA VI since 2015, while the art team grew far beyond GTA V’s to build a denser Vice City. The reporting adds production scale and map-planning context around the Nov. 19 release; Rockstar’s public launch date remains unchanged. @rockstargames
