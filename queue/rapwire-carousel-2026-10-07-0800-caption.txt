1) Young Thug (@youngthug) says his next solo album will be a Mary J. Blige-inspired R&B project planned for this year. He described most of the songs as focused on sex and heartbreak and also teased future collaborations with Drake, making this a sharp turn from his recent rap-heavy era.

2) Trippie Redd (@trippieredd) had his all-black South Florida mansion searched on Oct. 6 after Davie police assisted Miami investigators with a warrant. Authorities have not disclosed what they were seeking or announced charges tied to the search, so the legal picture remains open.

3) NBA YoungBoy (@nba_youngboy) says he wants to leave rap and focus more on his family. He also admitted music keeps pulling him back, which makes the statement less a retirement date than a candid look at how he is weighing the future of one of rap’s most prolific careers.

4) k-os (@kos) is back with “Guns & Roses,” his first new single in more than a decade and the lead-in to a new full-length. Drake is financing the comeback project, putting the Toronto veteran in a rare mentor-backed spotlight while the album details are still developing.

5) Xbox strategy chief Matthew Ball clarified that GTA VI will launch on Xbox Nov. 19 but will not stream exclusively or to PC. That closes the rumored day-one PC cloud workaround; Rockstar’s official launch listings still name PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with no native PC version announced.
