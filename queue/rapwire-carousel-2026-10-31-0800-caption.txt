1) Rick Ross (@richforever) no longer faces the felony battery-by-strangulation count prosecutors filed after his October 1 Miami Beach arrest. That charge was dropped for insufficient evidence, but a misdemeanor domestic-violence battery charge remains, and Ross has pleaded not guilty while the case continues.

2) LL COOL J (@llcoolj) is bringing ROCK THE BELLS: THE RESIDENCY to Dolby Live at Park MGM for 11 shows in May and July 2027. The all-star live experience starts May 15 and turns the history of hip-hop into a new Las Vegas stage run.

3) Hype Williams is set to receive the ToruńCAMERIMAGE Award for Achievements in Music Video in Poland this November. The honor recognizes a visual language that helped define hip-hop on screen through work with The Notorious B.I.G., Missy Elliott, Jay-Z, Busta Rhymes and more.

4) Takeoff’s mother and estate asked a Los Angeles judge to dismiss a civil sexual-assault lawsuit, arguing that the plaintiff deleted relevant text messages after filing the case. The request seeks terminating sanctions, but it is not a ruling; a hearing is scheduled for January 21, 2027, and the underlying allegations remain unresolved.

5) Rockstar says GTA VI’s Criminal Profile can move a player from Professional to Irredeemable, changing how the world reacts and influencing the story. The system is separate from Red Dead Redemption 2’s Honor meter, giving Jason and Lucia’s crimes a more persistent place in the game’s unfolding consequences.
