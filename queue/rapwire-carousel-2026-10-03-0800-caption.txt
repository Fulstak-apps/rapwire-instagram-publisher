1. Rick Ross (@richforever) surrendered in Miami Beach and faces felony battery-by-strangulation plus misdemeanor battery charges after an ex-partner’s allegations. He was released on $5,000 bond, pleaded not guilty, and has an Oct. 22 arraignment scheduled; the allegations remain unproven.

2. G Herbo (@gherbo) said on Instagram Live that he was retiring from rap because the business was draining, with acting and podcasting as possible next moves. He then walked that back in a follow-up, saying he ain’t ever quit at nothing, so the verified update is a reversal—not a retirement.

3. High Schoolers LLC, the trademark company controlled by André 3000 (@andre3000) and Big Boi (@bigboi), sued Oakland rapper-producer Ovrkast over the similar name and an alleged promise to rebrand as OVERKXST. The complaint seeks relief on trademark, unfair-competition, dilution and contract theories; no court has ruled on the allegations.

4. GTA VI is now listed with an ESRB M rating for Mature 17+ and descriptors including strong sexual content, nudity, violence, drugs and alcohol, language and mature humor. Separate regional ratings also specifically call out sex scenes, making the game’s adult-content profile clearer before its Nov. 19 release.

5. Rockstar opened preorders for a $399.99 Goodtime State–Vice City Collection inspired by Leonida’s fictional TV show Macca the Gator. The 11-piece box includes a gator figure, sunglasses, cap, bag, glassware, pins, stickers and map poster, but the GTA VI game is sold separately and the set ships Nov. 19.
