1) Rockstar Games and Atlantic Records announced *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, a 34-track original soundtrack arriving November 19 alongside the game. The first six singles are already out, including tracks from Travis Scott, Future, Metro Boomin, Yung Lean, PinkPantheress and Rauw Alejandro, giving the Vice City rollout a major music-world launch. https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire/article/7599a881942544/announcing-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-coming-november-19

2) Tee Grizzley released *They Forgot*, his new 16-track album, on September 25. The Detroit rapper’s rollout began with “Back Ina Yams,” and the full project arrives through 300 Entertainment with features including Ashanti and Skilla Baby. @TeeGrizzley https://music.apple.com/us/artist/tee-grizzley/1177271472

3) DDG’s 15-track album *HIT-A-THON* is out now through DDG Entertainment under license to Epic Records. The release expands the Pontiac rapper’s 2026 run with a full-length project built around his hit-making, creator-to-artist lane. @PontiacMadeDDG https://music.apple.com/us/album/hit-a-thon/6815843152

4) Mozzy and Trae tha Truth teamed up for *Still Tapped In*, their collaborative album released September 25 through ABN, Mozzy Records and EMPIRE. The Sacramento-Houston pairing makes the project a cross-regional statement as much as a new release. @Mozzy @TraeABN https://www.qobuz.com/us-en/album/still-tapped-in-trae-tha-truth-mozzy/t7se0newid9ri

5) Tinashe released *Popstar*, a 16-track album that moves through pop, R&B, dance and hip-hop without locking her into one lane. It is her first major-label album since 2018 and the next chapter after the independent run that included *333*, *BB/ANG3L* and *Quantum Baby*. @Tinashe https://pitchfork.com/story/tinashe-announces-new-album-popstar/
