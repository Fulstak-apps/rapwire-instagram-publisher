1) Toronto rapper SadBoi signed with Universal Music Canada on Nov. 24, adding a major-label chapter after winning the 2026 JUNO Rap Album/EP of the Year for DRY CRY. Her latest project, God Forbid I Have Fun., and her movement between rap, dance, reggae and baile funk make the deal a notable next step for one of Canada’s most flexible new voices. @whyusosadboi

2) GloRilla returned to Memphis on Nov. 24 for Big Glo’s 901 Turkey Drive at Smackers, where hundreds of families received Thanksgiving turkeys. The giveaway follows her $25,000 donation to Cordova High School and shows how the hometown rapper is turning a breakout year into visible local support. @glorillapimp

3) Spotify data shows U.S. streams of Texas rap climbed 125% from September 2021 to September 2026, the strongest growth among sizable Southern rap scenes. GTA VI’s radio soundtrack is now giving Houston classics another route to young listeners, but the five-year rise began before the game launched, so the moment is exposure—not proof that one title caused the trend.

4) Grand Theft Auto VI hides working QR codes in its world that can send a real phone to a parody Federal Investigation Bureau seizure notice on Rockstar’s website. The page rotates fake ads and legal charges, turning a bathroom poster into a joke that crosses from Leonida into real life; the in-game codes are safe, but unfamiliar real-world codes still deserve caution. @rockstargames

5) Rockstar’s launch-era developer notes explain that GTA VI’s HUD was modernized to stay useful without pulling players out of the world, while the studio also built a more individually authored population for Vice City. The update matters because the interface and the people around Jason and Lucia are both part of the game’s immersion, not just background decoration. @rockstargames
