1) Lil Baby (@lilbaby_) teams with Bally Baby and Fatt Smaxk on “Coast 2 Coast,” a new video that turns an Atlanta school bus into a moving party bus. The release also arrives with a Dec. 5 Lil Baby: Fresh Prince of Atlanta birthday show at State Farm Arena, giving the hometown campaign a live centerpiece.

2) Kid Rock says his upcoming Christian-rock album Halfway to Jesus, due Oct. 17, includes Eminem on “7 Sins.” Rock framed the collaboration as proof that their friendship survived political disagreement, making the song a high-profile reunion with an unusually charged context.

3) Doechii (@doechii) is part of Primavera Sound Barcelona 2027’s announced lineup, scheduled for June 2–6 at Parc del Fòrum. The festival has positioned her among the top-billed names in a 150-plus-act field, with the fan sale opening Oct. 8 and general tickets following Oct. 9.

4) Rockstar revealed six GTA VI radio stations and confirmed a first for the series: Jason and Lucia can listen to radio and on-demand podcasts while walking, using headphones or earbuds connected to their phones. The audio system broadens the game’s soundtrack beyond the car ahead of the Nov. 19 launch.

5) Mike D has released the “Secrets Pts. I & II” video from his debut album Thank You, which arrived Aug. 28 through Capitol Records. The official visual gives the project another push with a performance-led clip and keeps the rollout active beyond the album release itself.
