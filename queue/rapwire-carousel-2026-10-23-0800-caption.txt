1) @andersonpaak and @cordae release Heavy Is the Crown on Oct. 23, turning years of collaboration into their first full joint album. A Complex LA pop-up adds first listens, limited merch and a meet-and-signing, making the rollout a live fan event as well as a streaming drop.

2) @damedash’s life story is now part of a $550,000 bankruptcy auction win by Everlasting Films. The package includes worldwide story rights, about 100 hours of video, photos, memorabilia and an unreleased film project, while the proceeds are directed to creditors rather than Dash.

3) @iamkevingates has been ordered to route $1,079,341.50 from his music-catalog sale to Dreka Gates for child and spousal support and a direct payment. The remaining order allocates money for legal, accounting and mediation costs while their divorce dispute continues.

4) @asaprocky enters the final stretch before 2027 Grammy nominations after first-round voting closed Oct. 22. Early industry coverage places him in the contender conversation, while Drake is not expected in the nomination field; the nominees are scheduled for Nov. 16.

5) @rockstargames has taken GTA VI to broadcast television with a first commercial built from the launch trailer. The spot keeps Jason and Lucia at the center of the campaign and reinforces the Nov. 19 release on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
