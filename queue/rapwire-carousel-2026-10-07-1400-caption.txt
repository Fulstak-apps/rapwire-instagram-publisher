1) Will Smith (@willsmith) and DJ Jazzy Jeff are taking their pre-Fresh Prince story to scripted television with 215. The West Philadelphia-set series will dramatize the duo’s late-1980s climb toward a record deal and the mainstream, with Westbrook Studios and The Malar Group developing it; no cast, network, or premiere date has been announced.

2) Drake’s representative has denied online speculation that he is the father of Keshia Chanté’s unborn son, calling the rumor false. Chanté’s representative said the child’s father is a public figure in music but did not identify him, leaving the actual paternity private while the rumor cycle burns out.

3) Lil Durk’s separate federal racketeering case will not go to trial on the placeholder Oct. 5 date: U.S. District Judge Michael W. Fitzgerald moved it to Aug. 30, 2027. The defense cited the volume and complexity of the evidence, while Durk remains in federal custody after his acquittal in the earlier murder-for-hire trial and awaits a bond review.

4) Offset (@offsetyrn) has announced GMFU for Oct. 23, revealing the date while recapping Paris Fashion Week and framing the album as a return to music on his own terms. The tracklist and guest lineup are still under wraps, but the release keeps the Migos orbit active as Quavo’s new era and a Takeoff tribute reunion develop.

5) Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson says GTA VI will make money matter more to the story than previous Grand Theft Auto games. The full IGN interview, highlighted by GamesRadar+ and Insider Gaming, says Jason and Lucia may need to boost cars, rob businesses, and build cash before some missions can advance—turning the open world’s crime economy into a story gate.
