1) Gunna (@gunna) has released “Pop It,” a new single arriving with this Friday’s rap slate. The track is credited to Gunna Music and gives one of Atlanta’s biggest current names a fresh standalone release as the fall calendar tightens.

2) Ovrkast. released STRIKING, a 12-track album featuring Joey Bada$$, MAVI, Samara Cyn and Niontay. The Oakland rapper-producer’s release lands while High Schoolers LLC, the trademark company controlled by André 3000 and Big Boi, is suing over the Ovrkast. name. That makes the album both a new chapter and a real-time test of how the naming dispute affects his music and brand.

3) NetEase Cloud Music and gamma. announced a multi-year strategic partnership focused on music distribution in China. NetEase will serve as a launch platform for licensed recordings from gamma.’s roster, including Ye, Mariah Carey, Sexyy Red, Usher and Snoop Dogg. The deal matters because it puts a major China-market streaming service at the center of how that international catalog reaches listeners there.

4) Patrick Heaphy and LCM247 will premiere The Tupac Prison Interview on YouTube October 12 at 9 p.m. ET, featuring Heaphy’s extended 1995 conversation with Tupac Shakur at Clinton Correctional Facility. The premiere is timed to the 31st anniversary of Shakur’s release from the prison, and the original Sony camera and source tapes are planned for auction. It matters because the release adds a substantial archival window into Tupac at a pivotal point in his life.

5) Rockstar has made the official Grand Theft Auto VI Collection available through the Rockstar Store, with apparel and collectibles inspired by Vice City and Leonida. The drop is merchandise rather than the game itself, giving fans a physical pre-launch lane ahead of GTA VI’s November 19 release. It matters because Rockstar is turning the final month before launch into a wider retail campaign, not just a software countdown.
