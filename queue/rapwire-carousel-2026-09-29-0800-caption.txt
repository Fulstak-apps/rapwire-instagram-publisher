1) Meek Mill turned a leak into a release. After “Philly Heathens” and “Am I Wrong?” surfaced online, the Philadelphia rapper posted official video versions to YouTube, putting the music back under his control. The move also gave “Philly Heathens” a clear Philly-centered message about street violence and its fallout. @meekmill

2) 2 Chainz, Cash Cobain, and Young Nudy linked for “Reign,” a new single produced with Statik Selektah. The 3:05 track brings 2 Chainz’s veteran presence together with Cobain’s sexy-drill instincts and Nudy’s Atlanta drawl. It is a left-field collaboration that works as a snapshot of different rap generations meeting in one record. @2chainz @cash_cobain @youngnudy

3) Eminem’s long-rumored “Encore” Air Jordan 4 return has been pulled from Jordan Brand’s reported Summer 2027 calendar. The shoe’s status is still uncertain—delayed indefinitely or cancelled—with no confirmed replacement date. For collectors, that puts one of Em’s rarest collaborations back in limbo. @eminem

4) Rockstar reportedly reminded GTA VI voice performers to keep their involvement private under existing NDAs. The reported reminder follows Stephen Root confirming that he appears in the game, although he did not identify his character. Rockstar is still holding most of the cast details until the November 19 launch window. @rockstargames

5) Rockstar’s $399.99 Goodtime State–Vice City Collection is a merchandise box, not a copy of GTA VI—the game is sold separately. The set includes items such as sunglasses, a cap, a map, pins, and a gator figure, and Ubisoft’s Rayman account used that omission for a fresh joke about its own launch edition. The episode shows how GTA VI’s marketing is becoming news before release. @rockstargames @ubisoft
