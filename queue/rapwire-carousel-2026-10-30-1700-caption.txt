1) Sampa the Great (@sampathegreat) released her third album, Nu Zamrock, on Oct. 30 through Loma Vista. The Zambian-Australian rapper draws on family history and Zamrock influence for a new chapter after a four-year gap.
2) GloRilla (@glorillapimp) and Sexyy Red (@sexyyred) are bringing their next collaboration, Jiggly Bow Bow, on Nov. 6 through CMG and Interscope. The single extends a proven Memphis–St. Louis pairing with a fresh release-day moment.
3) Rockstar Games says Justice’s Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score will arrive Dec. 11 on streaming services, CD and vinyl. The 30-track project includes the already released “Pusher” with Gesaffelstein, giving the game a dedicated musical identity beyond its radio soundtrack.
4) The Prince Estate and NPG Records announced a 3121 Expanded Edition for Dec. 11 with ten vault recordings, plus a 154-minute Prince: 3121 Las Vegas Concert film opening Dec. 3 in U.S. and Canadian theaters. The anniversary campaign revisits the 2006 album and footage from Prince’s 2007 Las Vegas residency.
5) Ken Carson (@kencarson) is taking over Roblox for Halloween with virtual concerts in The Block on Oct. 30 and 31 at 4 p.m. Pacific. Tie-ins across The Mimic, Blade Ball and Fisch turn the Atlanta rapper’s live rollout into a playable fan event.
