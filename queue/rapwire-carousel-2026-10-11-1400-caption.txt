4) Drake and Don Toliver’s “Solar Eclipse” now has an official music video, released alongside “Cold Shoulder” from the FOMO rollout. Complex reports that both songs first appeared in Drake’s short film before arriving on the expanded “HABIBTI (FOMO)” edition, and Apple Music lists the standalone visual. @champagnepapi @dontoliver

5) DistroKid confirmed to The Verge that a small number of recordings were removed in response to claims made by Universal Music Group, while saying it disputes the label’s lawsuit. Artists have reported missing releases and little notice or recourse, and the federal case remains allegations rather than a ruling. The dispute matters because a distributor’s enforcement choices can take independent music offline before a court decides the underlying claims.
