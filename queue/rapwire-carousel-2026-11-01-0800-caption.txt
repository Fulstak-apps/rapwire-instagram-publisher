1) 2 Chainz (@2chainz) and Statik Selektah have set Dec. 4 for Playaz Only Live Once, a new 18-track album produced entirely by Statik. “A Week In Paris” is out now, giving fans the clearest preview yet of a focused rapper-producer collaboration instead of a one-off feature.

2) GloRilla (@glorillapimp) and Sexyy Red (@sexyyred) will release “Jiggly Bow Bow” on Nov. 6 through Yo Gotti’s CMG and Interscope. The pair teased the track before revealing the cover art, extending a run of collaborations that has already included “WHATCHU KNO ABOUT ME,” “Sticky,” “Baller” and “Call Your Name.”

3) Megan Thee Stallion (@theestallion) has packaged her Tina Snow run into the 3-track Tina Tuesday Freestyles EP. It collects “Tina Tuesday,” “Is You Cool?” and “Thin Ice,” and keeps the Houston rapper’s alter ego in focus as she prepares another album expected by year’s end.

4) Timbaland (@timbaland) filed a 65-page Los Angeles complaint seeking more than $15 million from former manager Gary Marella and former business manager Bruce Seckendorf. The suit alleges fraud, civil theft and fiduciary breaches tied to commissions, expenses and business finances; those claims are allegations, not a court finding.

5) Rockstar Games developers are scheduled to vote on union recognition from Nov. 12 through Nov. 26, placing the ballot across GTA VI’s pre-load week and its Nov. 19 console launch. If approved, the IWGB Rockstar Game Workers Union would become the biggest recognized game-industry union in the UK, with a formal voice on pay, overtime, layoffs and workplace policy.
