1) Grand Theft Auto VI just posted an estimated $3.79 billion in worldwide day-one retail sales after moving 32.5 million units before launch. Rockstar calls it the biggest entertainment launch across any medium, while the estimate is based on the company's Nov. 19 results. @rockstargames

2) Rockstar's Nov. 23 launch update fixes crashes, broken autosaves, story-progress phone calls, stuck Wanted Levels and weather that stopped changing. It is an early quality-of-life pass for a game whose bugs could otherwise interrupt missions or erase progress. @rockstargames

3) Houston's sound is getting another national moment. Spotify data shows U.S. Texas-rap streams climbed 125% from September 2021 to September 2026, while Mike Jones' “Still Tippin'” and “Back Then” are in GTA VI's radio mix and are driving fresh attention. The five-year rise predates GTA VI, so the game is an amplifier—not the sole cause. @where_mikejones

4) Netflix premieres The Tupac Tapes with Angie Martinez on Nov. 27, a four-part video podcast built around unreleased portions of her 1996 interview with Tupac Shakur. Martinez held the material for nearly three decades, and the series adds voices reflecting on the rapper's life and legacy. @angiemartinez

5) Rockstar's official credits confirm Denzel Curry voices DWNPLY, an in-game rival rapper in GTA VI. The same credits list three DWNPLY songs—“Bulletproof Ski Mask,” “Pax” and “Politik”—turning the role into a music-and-performance crossover, not just a cameo. @denzelcurry @rockstargames
